Public input dictionary (definitions, not an intervention policy)

Timing and history: day=t is the decision index, 0..29. Its observation contains only information available before the next simulated market day. Your action affects the next close t+1, not a past close. Up to five observations are ordered oldest to newest. history_mask distinguishes unavailable initial frames; missing frames are not zero-valued observations. interventions contains all executed FREEZE actions in this episode, not proposed actions. No previous episode or hidden conversational memory is supplied.

Permissions: action_targets lists all three legal asset identifiers. action_mask corresponds to WAIT followed by those three targets. remaining_budget is administrative capacity, initially 12; WAIT costs 0 and each one-day FREEZE costs 4. There is no reward for spending the budget or quota requiring all three freezes. FREEZE is the same full-strength bundle described above: restrictions on associated messages and trade/spoof/wash orders, automatically released after one market day. It can affect ordinary participants too. No entity-targeted action is available. Asset/entity IDs are opaque references, stable within an episode; a list rank is not an identity. society_size is participant count; information_level labels the public observation tier, not risk severity.

Asset fields: return_3d is the price change divided by its starting price over up to the last three days (0.01 means 1%). volatility_3d is the population standard deviation of up to three one-day fractional returns, not annualized. volume_zscore is (latest volume - mean of the preceding up to three volumes)/(their population standard deviation + 0.000001), clipped to [-10,10]; it is zero when fewer than three volume records exist. This short-window statistic is not a calibrated tail probability, and a clip value does not identify an attack. order_imbalance is the latest displayed order-book depth imbalance. cancellation_rate is the latest cancellation count divided by max(order count,1).

Market fields: return_1d is the mean latest fractional daily return across assets. return_3d, volatility_3d, volume_zscore, order_imbalance and cancellation_rate are arithmetic means of the corresponding per-asset measures, not total-volume-weighted statistics. liquidity is the mean of 1/max(current spread/base spread,1), a relative spread proxy in [0,1], not cash or available execution capacity. Early-history statistics use the records available; zero is not a certified normal-state label.

Social fields: message_rate is the sum of the latest per-asset message-volume/intensity statistics divided by society size, not the count of messages shown below. cascade_reach is the current cascade audience union divided by the number of participants, clipped to [0,1]. sentiment_concentration is the message-volume-weighted absolute sentiment, clipped to [0,1]. network_degree_concentration and information_concentration are normalized Herfindahl indices of nonnegative network degrees and per-person absolute exposure magnitude: (sum of squared shares - 1/n)/(1 - 1/n), or zero if the population/total is insufficient. consensus_concentration is abs(sum of signed per-person exposures)/sum of their absolute signed exposures, or zero if its denominator is zero. These are descriptive statistics, not probabilities of harm.

Entities: at most five are shown, selected by descending score = message_reach + trading_concentration + cancellation_rate + social_influence + min(activity_change/2,1), with ID breaking ties. They are selected extremes, not five random participants; membership can change. message_reach is the entity's share of current total potential message reach. trading_concentration is its share of submitted order quantity, not executed turnover. cancellation_rate is its cancellation count/max(order count,1). social_influence is network degree/max network degree. These four fields are clipped to [0,1]. activity_change is current activity divided by mean activity in up to three preceding records, clipped to [0,10]; activity = nonnegative message intensity + nonnegative order count. If the previous mean is effectively zero, it is 10 for positive current activity and 1 for zero activity. It is a ratio, not a z-score. An extreme displayed entity need not imply an extreme market-wide cancellation rate.

Message summary: the source public list selects at most eight messages from the latest three days, newest first, then larger realized audience reach, with public identifiers breaking ties. For each asset, count is the number in that selected list; audience_reach is the SUM of each selected message's audience-size/society-size fraction, without deduplicating recipients, so it can exceed 1; present distinguishes a selected zero-reach message from no selected message. These are not exhaustive market message counts. The input omits message text and individual sender-message links.

The input does not supply private roles, manipulation targets, reference-world accounts, future outcomes or counterfactual wealth rewards. No numerical intervention threshold or attack timetable is specified by this dictionary.
